# Top 50 Fragrance Sites Worldwide — Catalog Depth

Researched August 2026 across four parallel passes: US/Canada, Europe, Middle
East/Asia/Oceania, and reference databases.

**How to read the numbers.** Every figure is tagged. **Verified** came from a
live category counter, a filter facet, a Shopify `collections.json`
`products_count`, or a "showing X of Y" string. **Claim** is the site's own
marketing copy. **Unverified** means the site renders counts in JavaScript and
no real number could be extracted — those are left blank rather than guessed.

**The counting trap.** Most sites publish SKUs, not distinct fragrances. One
fragrance becomes 30ml / 50ml / 100ml / refill / gift set = 4-6 SKUs. The ratio
runs roughly 2.5-4x on mass retailers and 1.5-2x on niche shops. So a
9,000-SKU fragrance category is realistically 2,500-3,500 distinct wearable
fragrances. Where a site's number includes candles, body care or skincare,
that's flagged.

## ⚠️ Five corrections to earlier notes in this project

1. **Fragrance Direct is dead.** fragrancedirect.co.uk shows "making changes
   behind the scenes" and redirects shoppers to allbeauty.com. It was #3 on the
   Awin day-one shortlist in `AWIN_TARGETS.md`. **Remove it.**
2. **Douglas's "140,000+ articles" is the whole store, not fragrance.** Their
   actual Parfum category is **5,759**. Flaconi's is **5,566**, not 50,000.
   Those bigger numbers are store-wide and include all beauty.
3. **Notino is the deepest fragrance catalog in Europe**, at 9,413 (DE) /
   8,982 (UK). That is ~60% deeper than Douglas. It's on **CJ**, which weakens
   the "Awin first, CJ later" ordering.
4. **Notino UK serves English product titles.** If you get a Notino feed,
   request the .co.uk or .com market specifically and the entire German-title
   matching problem disappears while keeping the deepest catalog in Europe.
5. **Fragrantica is not the biggest database. Parfumo is**, at 230,907 verified
   from their own live counter. And there *is* a legitimately licensable
   database, which earlier research missed. See the database section.

**Parfumo beats Fragrantica on catalog but they count differently.** Parfumo
splits by concentration and edition, so its 230K is closer to SKU-level;
Fragrantica groups at the fragrance level. FragDB's cross-walk of the two found
only **83,643 matched pairs** out of 132K and 220K, meaning each holds a huge
tail the other doesn't. Neither is a superset.

### The licensing answer earlier research missed

**Fragrances of the World** (Michael Edwards, Australia, since 1984) is the one
legitimately licensable fragrance database. It is explicitly "API driven," sells
pre-formatted and custom feeds to retailers, and runs white-label corporate
instances (e.g. `symrise.fragrancesoftheworld.info`). **It is the data behind
Puig's Wikiparfum.** ~63,000 records, classified first-party by Edwards' own
evaluation team on the Fragrance Wheel, updated daily. Pricing is quote-based,
behind a sales contact.

Its weakness is the long tail: it's curated and current-market focused, so it's
thinner than Parfumo on vintage, discontinued and obscure releases. The
realistic architecture is a licensed FOTW core plus an internally-built tail.

**Parfumo will not license at any price.** Founder Christoph Polatzky has
publicly stated he has rejected multiple e-commerce inquiries for database
access and declined a major retailer's request to republish reviews. Solo
operator, volunteer moderators, deliberately anti-monetization. Their robots.txt
blocks ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Bytespider and Amazonbot, and they publish
`Content-Signal: ai-train=no`.

**FragDB and Fragella, with receipts.** FragDB now self-documents its
provenance: "Fragrantica core (132K perfumes)" plus "Parfumo bundle preview
(219,963 perfumes)," 4,643,851 user reviews, 24,440 editorial articles. It is
reselling *both* leaders' scraped content including user reviews neither party
licensed to it, at inconsistent prices ($200/$400 one-time on GitHub vs
$1,000/yr on their site), crypto only, no company registration disclosed.
Fragella's own description is an admission: aggregates from "public sources,
fragrance communities" then uses "a proprietary machine learning model" to
"predict and fill in missing data points." That is scraped data with
**synthetically invented values mixed in** and presented as fact. For an
encyclopedia, that's a correctness hazard on top of a copyright one.

**AI-era newcomers all cluster suspiciously around Fragrantica's true size:**
Perfume Finder 125,000+, MyPerfumeAI 120,000+, ScentWise 75,000+. None discloses
licensing or acquisition method. A 120-125K count with no stated source is a
Fragrantica scrape.

## Where the Arab houses actually live

This is the 15-20% of the catalog no Western network covers, and the answer is
better than "nowhere."

**The Oud Store** (`oudstore.com`) is the find. 2,052 products, Shopify, English
titles, and the deepest verified brand collections anywhere: **Lattafa 339,
Fragrance World 279, Maison Alhambra 191, Emper 100, Armaf 91, Ard Al Zaafaran
84, Volare 55, Grandeur 50, Al Wataniah 48, Al Haramain 45**. More Lattafa SKUs
than any networked retailer verified. No affiliate program, but Shopify makes a
private feed trivial.

**My Perfume Shop (AU)** is the best depth-plus-reachability combination:
Armaf 235, Ajmal 130, Afnan 71 inside an 11,341-product store, on **Commission
Factory** at 6.5% / 30 days covering AU, NZ, **UAE** and UK from one signup.
Commission Factory was missing from earlier network research and shouldn't have
been.

**Faces** (ArabClicks, 7 GCC markets) has Arabian Oud 152, Maison El Nabil 148,
Lattafa 120, Ramasat 93.

**Direct brand stores** are small but canonical for correct naming: Rasasi 337,
Ajmal India 302, Lattafa USA 286, Swiss Arabian 251.

**Regional network notes.** ArabClicks is the real Gulf network (500+ programs,
carries Ajmal, Faces, Strawberrynet, Ounass, Namshi). Three cautions: Noon is
**coupon-code tracking only**, no cookies, so it's unmonetizable for a
click-driven app; **Golden Scent's program is PAUSED**, so the biggest claimed
Arabic catalog has no open door; and **no ArabClicks program page mentions a
product feed** — Admitad UAE lists 209 advertisers with feeds and is the better
regional feed source. India runs on Cuelinks and Admitad India; SE Asia on
Involve Asia.

## Dead, moved, or broken — don't waste time

- **Fragrance Direct** (UK) — redirects to allbeauty. Awin 9 is legacy.
- **Feelunique** — absorbed into Sephora UK.
- **Beautyspin** — redirects to Notino, which **exited the US market**. Notino's
  83,000 products are EU-only.
- **Parfum1** — shut down January 2020.
- **Hudson's Bay** — liquidated 2025, IP bought by Canadian Tire.
- **MiN New York** — single-house brand site, not a retailer.
- **Paris Gallery** — domain moved, `/en/fragrance` 404s, appears rebranded.
- **Rivoli** — watch retailer, not fragrance depth.
- **Arabian Oud** — `arabianoud.com/en-ae` returns 410 Gone; live on Salla at
  `sa.arabianoud.com`, no public counts, no affiliate program.
- **Al Haramain** — no canonical store; bare domain 404s, `shop.` and `us.`
  subdomains are separate Magento instances.
- **Iperprofumi** — DNS fails on both .com and .it.
