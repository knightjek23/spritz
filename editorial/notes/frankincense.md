The resin itself, before it becomes smoke. Bright and peppery up top, with a clean lemon-pine sparkle that most people do not expect from a resin, settling into a dry, mineral, faintly balsamic warmth. Where "incense" in a perfume usually means the burning, smoky accord, frankincense on its own reads cooler and lighter: more air than ash. It gives a composition lift and a quiet, meditative backbone without weighing it down.
