Smoky, dry, slightly lemony at the edges, like the inside of an old cathedral. The burning accord rather than any one resin: usually built around frankincense with a little myrrh or labdanum, it has a meditative, cold quality that cuts through sweeter notes and gives a fragrance weight without adding warmth.
